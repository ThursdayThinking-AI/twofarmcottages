We moved to Amberley in June 2002 after living in the Lake District for five years, where we ran a small guest house near Cockermouth.

We enjoy meeting people and have been overwhelmed at how nice our guests have been, and we must have a winning formula after 20 years, as all the comments in the visitors’ book prove!

We moved to Two Farm Cottages in 2015 after living at Stream Cottage in the old part of the village for thirteen years. 16th-century thatched cottages certainly look pretty, but you need banker’s bonuses to maintain them.

Our new home is much younger, originally one of a pair of farm cottages built about 1890 and extended in 1990. The views of the Downs to the south and the Wildbrooks to the north are a delight and never fail to encourage some excellent time-wasting gazing.

If you are unfamiliar with Amberley then brace yourself for a real treat. Only 54 miles from London, it is known as the ‘Pearl of Sussex’, with many picturesque lanes, houses, gardens and a 12th-century church and castle.

We pride ourselves on our hospitality and ability to make guests feel at home – the breakfast table overflows with different ideas that change every month and has been nominated for an excellence award by the AA and a WOW award from many guests. There are many good pubs and restaurants in the area, or light evening snacks are often available here at the cottage.

Janet is also a qualified reflexologist, so if any of the wonderful local walks are just a bit too much, professional help is at hand.
