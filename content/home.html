Amberley is one of the picturesque show-villages of southern England, set within the South Downs National Park. It sits at the foot of the Downs, close to the Arun Gap and the Wildbrooks water meadows. Two Farm Cottages is a delightful home with stunning views of the Downs to the south and the water meadows to the north. It was originally one of a pair of farm cottages built around 1890 and carefully extended one hundred years later. Connie, our Great Dane, Mack and Mabel the two cats, and Janet and Mike, the resident adults, all offer you a warm welcome and a very comfortable stay.

Our Comfortable Rooms

We have two beautiful rooms in part of the original cottage, completely secure and private: a double facing the Downs and a twin-bedded room looking towards the Wildbrooks. Ideal for families or groups of friends travelling together. There is no sharing stairwells or the breakfast table with total strangers. A private bathroom for guests is equipped with a shower and a bath for soaking tired legs. TV and tea/coffee making facilities with free WiFi are all supplied.
