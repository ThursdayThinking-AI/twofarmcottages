The property lies close to the town of Arundel, with its commanding 11th-century castle, and the beautiful city of Chichester, which is dominated by the impressive cathedral and home to the internationally renowned Festival Theatre. There are historic houses at Parham and Petworth and Roman ruins at Bignor and Fishbourne. The Wildfowl and Wetlands Trust at Arundel and the RSPB reserve at Pulborough are worth a visit, with Goodwood and the coast within a short drive through countryside that has inspired the words of Kipling, Tennyson and Shelley.
